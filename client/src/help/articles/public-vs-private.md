# What's the difference between public and private leagues?

The visibility setting controls who can see your league and how new members join.

## Public leagues

- Standings, games, and stats are visible to anyone — no account required.
- Anyone can find your league's join page and request to join.
- You (the owner or admin) approve or deny each request from Settings.
- Great for open community leagues or when you want to show off the standings publicly.

## Private leagues

- Nothing is visible to non-members — the league doesn't appear in any public listing.
- New members join only via a private invite link you share with them.
- They're added automatically when they tap the link.
- Great for closed groups, family leagues, or any situation where you control access tightly.

## Switching visibility

You can switch between public and private any time in **Settings → League Info → Visibility**. Existing members aren't affected.
