# What's the difference between Hamilton Rules and ACA?

Both are valid ways to play cornhole — they just produce different scoring rhythms.

## Hamilton Rules

Hamilton Rules use **cancellation scoring**: each team's bags cancel out the other's before points are added to the running total. Only the net score from a round gets added.

- Hole = 3 points, Board = 1 point (same as ACA)
- Points cancel between teams each frame
- First team to **21 wins** (win by 1)

This tends to produce longer, more strategic games because big rounds can cancel each other out.

## ACA (American Cornhole Association)

ACA uses **count-all scoring**: every bag counts, no cancellation. Each team adds up their own points separately and the running totals grow independently.

- Hole = 3 points, Board = 1 point
- No cancellation — all bags score
- First team to **21 wins** (win by 1)

ACA games tend to progress faster and swing bigger.

## Which should I use?

Neither is "right" — it depends on what your group is used to. Hamilton Rules are common in the Ontario backyard scene; ACA is standard at organised competitive events.

You can switch your league's rules any time in **Settings → League Info → Scoring Rules**. If you want something completely custom, Pro leagues can configure their own point values, target score, and more.
