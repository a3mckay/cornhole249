# What happens when my Weekend Pass expires?

Weekend Pass gives you 7 days of full Pro access. Here's what happens when it runs out.

## At expiry

- Your league reverts to the **Free plan**.
- Pro features (tournaments, stats, custom rules, exports) become locked.
- All your data — games, stats, standings — is preserved. Nothing is deleted.
- If you had more than 8 members while on the pass, your league enters a **grace period** to manage access.

## The 8-member grace period

The Free plan allows up to 8 members. If you have more than 8 when the pass expires:

- You get a **7-day grace period** to choose which 8 members keep access.
- During this window, everyone can still use the league.
- After the grace period, access is locked for members beyond the 8 oldest.
- You'll see a panel in **Settings** to choose which 8 to keep.

## Renewing

You can renew your Weekend Pass or upgrade to Pro any time from **Settings → Plan**.

- Another Weekend Pass gives you 7 more days.
- Pro Monthly or Yearly gives ongoing access with no expiry worries.
