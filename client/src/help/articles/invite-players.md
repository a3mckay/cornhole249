# How do I invite players to my league?

Getting your crew into the app takes about 30 seconds. Here's how.

## Private leagues — invite link

If your league is set to **Private**, go to **Settings → Invite Link**. You'll see a shareable link and QR code. Send it by SMS, copy it, or let people scan the QR code. Anyone who taps the link and has an account is added to your league automatically.

The invite link is stable — it doesn't expire unless you reset it manually. You can reset it from Settings if you want to cut off access to the old link.

## Public leagues — join page

If your league is **Public**, anyone can find it and request to join at `/l/your-league/join`. You'll get a notification in Settings when there's a pending request, and you can approve or deny it there.

## Sharing the QR code

There's also a **QR Share** button in the bottom-right corner on desktop, or in the share menu on mobile. Great for printing and sticking it to the board.
