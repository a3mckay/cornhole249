# How do refunds work?

We want you to be happy with Cornhole249. If something went wrong, here's what to expect.

## Our refund policy

- **Pro Monthly / Pro Yearly**: If you're not satisfied within the first 7 days of a new subscription, contact us and we'll refund you in full, no questions asked.
- **Weekend Pass**: Because Weekend Pass access is immediate and the window is short, Weekend Passes are generally non-refundable — but if you had a technical issue that prevented you from using it, reach out and we'll work something out.
- **Renewals**: We don't offer refunds on renewal charges once the billing period has started. Cancel before your renewal date to avoid being charged.

## How to request a refund

[Contact us](/help/contact) with your league name and the email address on your account. We process refunds within 2–3 business days. Stripe typically posts the refund to your card within 5–10 business days.

## Cancelling vs. refunding

Cancelling your subscription stops future charges but doesn't refund the current period. If you want a refund for the current period, you need to contact us — cancelling alone won't trigger one.
