# How do I recover my PIN?

PIN login is a legacy feature from an earlier version of the app and is being phased out. New accounts use email + password.

## If you still use a PIN

PINs cannot be recovered — they were never tied to an email address. If you've forgotten your PIN and can't log in, the best path forward is to **claim your account** with an email address and password.

## Claiming your account

If you can log in with your PIN:

1. Go to the banner at the top of the page that says **"PIN login is being phased out."**
2. Click **Set up email + password →**
3. Enter your email address and create a password.

Once you've claimed your account, you can log in with email + password from any device and reset your password if you ever forget it.

## If you're locked out completely

If you've forgotten your PIN and can't get in, [contact us](/help/contact) and we'll help you regain access. Include the display name on your account so we can find it.
