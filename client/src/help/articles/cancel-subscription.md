# How do I cancel my subscription?

You can cancel your Pro subscription any time — no hoops, no hidden steps.

## Cancelling from the app

1. Go to **Settings** for your league.
2. Under **Plan**, click **Manage Subscription**.
3. You'll be taken to the Stripe customer portal.
4. Click **Cancel plan** and follow the prompts.

Your league stays on Pro until the end of the current billing period. After that, it reverts to Free.

## What happens after cancellation

- Pro features lock at the end of your billing period.
- Your data is preserved — games, stats, standings are all still there.
- If you have more than 8 members, a grace period starts to manage access.
- You can re-subscribe any time from Settings.

## Weekend Pass

Weekend Passes aren't subscriptions — they're one-time purchases that expire automatically after 7 days. There's nothing to cancel.

## Need a refund?

Cancelling doesn't automatically trigger a refund. See [How do refunds work?](/help/refunds) if you'd like to request one.
