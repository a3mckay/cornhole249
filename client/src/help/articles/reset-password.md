# How do I reset my password?

Forgot your password? No problem — here's how to get back in.

## From the login page

1. Go to [/login](/login) and click **Forgot password?**
2. Enter your email address and click **Send reset link**.
3. Check your inbox for an email from Cornhole249.
4. Click the link in the email — it takes you to a page to set a new password.
5. Log in with your new password.

The reset link expires after a short window for security. If it's expired, just request a new one.

## Tips

- Check your spam folder if the email doesn't arrive within a minute or two.
- The reset email is sent from `noreply.cornhole249@gmail.com`.
- If you don't have an email address on your account yet (legacy PIN user), see [How do I recover my PIN?](/help/recover-pin)
