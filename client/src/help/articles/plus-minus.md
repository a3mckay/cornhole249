# Why does my +/- look different than I expect?

The +/- (plus-minus) stat shows your net point differential across all games — points scored minus points allowed. It's a useful signal for how dominant your wins are and how close your losses are, not just whether you won.

## How it's calculated

For each game:
- If you scored 21 and your opponent scored 15, your +/- for that game is **+6**.
- If you scored 12 and your opponent scored 21, your +/- is **−9**.

Your cumulative +/- adds up all of these across every game you've played this season.

## Common surprises

**"I have a winning record but negative +/-."**  
This can happen if your wins are narrow (e.g. 21–20 each time) and your losses are large. You're winning the right games but getting blown out when you lose.

**"My +/- is huge but my win rate is lower than someone else's."**  
You're winning by bigger margins. Both stats together give the full picture.

**"The number changed after I logged a game that was already in the past."**  
Stats update in real time when a game is logged, regardless of when the game was played. Logging old games can shift historical +/-.
