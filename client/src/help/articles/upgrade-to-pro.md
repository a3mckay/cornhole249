# How do I upgrade to Pro?

Pro unlocks the full feature set: tournaments, advanced stats, custom rules, no player cap, and more.

## Upgrading

1. Go to **Settings** for your league.
2. Under **Plan**, click **Upgrade to Pro**.
3. Choose **Pro Monthly**, **Pro Yearly**, or **Weekend Pass**.
4. Complete checkout via Stripe — your league upgrades instantly.

Only the league owner or an admin can manage billing.

## Plan options

| Plan | Price | Best for |
|------|-------|----------|
| Pro Monthly | $X/mo | Ongoing leagues |
| Pro Yearly | $X/yr | Best value, ~2 months free |
| Weekend Pass | $X one-time | 7 days of Pro for events/tournaments |

## What's included in Pro?

- **Tournaments** — bracket and round-robin formats
- **Stats** — advanced per-player and per-team analytics
- **Custom rules** — configure your own scoring
- **Custom theme** — league colours and logo
- **Exports** — download your data as CSV
- **No 8-player cap** — add as many members as you need

## Cancelling

You can cancel any time from **Settings → Plan → Manage Subscription**. You keep Pro access until the end of your current billing period.
